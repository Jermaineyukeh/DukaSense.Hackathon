How DukaSense works

Gross profit, not net profit

Gross profit is revenue minus estimated cost of goods. Rent, wages, transport and electricity are not included.

Rules, not a model

Recommendations come from simple, explainable rules: stock cover against recent sales, margin against a threshold, and the slow-moving flag in your data. There is no trained model and nothing is forecast.

Stock is a snapshot

Stock levels come from one inventory snapshot and do not change with the date filter. Units sold and margin follow the dates you pick.

Your data stays in memory

An uploaded file is checked, held in memory and cleared when you restart the app. Every number on screen is calculated by the server.
